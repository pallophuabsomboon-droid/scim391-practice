Privacy notice — SCIM 391 Python Practice

This website is a practice tool for the course SCIM 391 Data Structure in Mathematics, Department of Mathematics, Faculty of Science, Mahidol University. It is run by the course instructor.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your e-mail address and your name.
  • Each time you sign in: the date and time.
  • Each time you press Run tests: the code you submitted, which tests passed, and the time.

Why

Only to give you feedback on practice problems and to record course scores (assignments). Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for any other purpose.

Who can see it

You can see your own records. The course instructor and teaching assistants can see the records of all students in the course. The data is stored in a Supabase database.

How long

Records are kept only as long as needed for the course and for the university's record-keeping rules.

Contact

Questions or requests to see or delete your data: Asst. Prof. Dr. Pallop Huabsomboon, pallop.hua@mahidol.ac.th.
